# The Four Information Layers of Sanskruti

Sanskriti enforces strict separation between four categories of knowledge to preserve indigenous epistemic integrity:

### Layer A: VERIFIED / ARCHIVAL KNOWLEDGE
- **Source**: Primary museum accessions (National Crafts Museum, IGNCA, Calico Museum of Textiles), peer-reviewed academic monographs, archaeological records.
- **Badge**: Emerald / Gold Seal (`VERIFIED`).
- **Standard**: Must include Title, Author/Researcher, Year, and Institution citation.

### Layer B: COMMUNITY / PRACTITIONER KNOWLEDGE
- **Source**: Living master artisans, hereditary lineages, and indigenous communities.
- **Badge**: Sky / Cyan Seal (`COMMUNITY`).
- **Standard**: Captures oral histories, generational recipes, and sacred ritual rules that formal texts omit.

### Layer C: AI INTERPRETATION
- **Source**: AI intelligence synthesized using Layers A & B as strict grounding context (RAG).
- **Badge**: Purple / Amethyst Seal (`AI_INTERPRETATION`).
- **Standard**: Explicitly marks responses as AI interpretation, cites the underlying archival sources, and displays referenced catalog items.

### Layer D: AI-GENERATED CREATIVE CONTENT
- **Source**: Algorithmic generative image or text models.
- **Badge**: Amber / Orange Seal (`AI_GENERATED`).
- **Standard**: Strictly distinguished from authentic heritage artifacts. Never presented as authentic traditional artifacts.
